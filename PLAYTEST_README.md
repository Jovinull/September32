# September 32 — Act 1, private playtest

Thank you for reading this before anybody else has.

This is the first week of the story. It ends where Act 1 ends, which is on
purpose — there is no more written yet.

## How to play it

**Play naturally.** Choose what you would actually choose. That is the whole
thing being tested.

**There is no optimal route.** Nothing is scored, nothing is unlocked, and
nothing is missable in a way you will regret.

**Do not restart because you think you chose wrong.** There is no wrong. If a
choice turns out badly, that is the game working.

**You are not expected to see everything.** The week is assembled from more
scenes than one playthrough contains, so you will miss some. Everybody does.

**Save and load however you normally would.** Rolling back with the mouse wheel
works, and so does the Back button.

**Take as long as you like.** Most people should expect somewhere around an hour
to an hour and a half; a fast reader will be quicker, and stopping halfway and
coming back is fine.

## Two things to know before you start

**Some visual assets are temporary and should not be judged as final art.** You
will see a few images that are obviously placeholders and at least one that is a
labelled grey box. That is a production gap, not a style. There is a question
about it on the questionnaire.

**The game will ask, once, whether it may keep a report on your computer.**
Either answer is fine and the game is identical either way. If you say yes it
writes down which scenes you saw and which options you took, so that what you
remember can be read next to what actually happened. Nothing is uploaded, ever;
there is no network code in this game at all. Details are in
`FEEDBACK_INSTRUCTIONS.md`.

## Afterwards

**Please answer the questionnaire before you play again.** Your first reaction
is the part that cannot be recovered later, and it is the most useful thing you
can give us. Replay as much as you like afterwards.

`FEEDBACK_INSTRUCTIONS.md`, next to this file, says what to send and where.

## If something breaks

Tell us. A crash, a scene that repeats oddly, a line that names something you
never saw, text that runs off the screen — all of it is worth reporting, and
none of it is your fault. There is a short form in the feedback instructions.
