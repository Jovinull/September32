## Notes

Interface and dialogue face. Chosen for a large x-height that survives a
dialogue box at 1080p, and for complete Latin coverage including every
diacritic pt-BR needs. TTF over OTF for the widest engine support.

The OFL requires the licence text to travel with the font, so LICENSE.md is
copied alongside the .ttf files at import time and reproduced in
THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md.
