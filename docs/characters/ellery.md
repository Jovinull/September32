# Ms. Ellery

**Status:** `PROVISIONAL`
**Data file:** `data/characters/ellery.json`

## Function

**Role**
Faculty. Supervises the archive digitisation. Decides what gets catalogued and
what gets a box at the back.

**Narrative function**
The institution, with a face. Not a conspiracy — a person with a filing system, a
budget, and a job that has outlasted several versions of the school. She is where
the story asks who decides what is worth keeping.

## Interior

**Public personality**
Pleasant, unhurried, entirely unbothered by urgency. Answers a question with the
part of it that has an answer.

**Private personality**
Undecided, deliberately. `IDEA` — whether she knows anything is the biggest open
question about her, and settling it early would close down her best scenes.

**Core desire.** For the archive to be finished and correct. Probably.
**Core fear.** Not decided.
**Core contradiction.** She gave the project to a student and then left her alone
with it for eight months. That was either trust or disposal, and Iris has not
worked out which either.

**Values.** Process. Continuity. Not making a fuss.
**Boundaries.** Not yet found. Finding one is a scene worth writing.

## Voice

**Speech patterns.** Measured, complete, slightly formal. Never hurried. Ends a
conversation by having finished, not by leaving.

**Vocabulary.** Administrative and precise. Uses the institution's own words for
things, which is occasionally revealing.

**Humour.** Dry, infrequent, and it lands — which is always slightly surprising.

**Topics avoided.** Unknown. Finding out what she deflects is the scene.

**Under stress.** Unknown. Nobody has seen it.
**When comfortable.** Unknown.

## Relationships

**Ellery → player.** Formal. She is a member of staff, and the game should not
forget it.
**Ellery → Iris.** The relationship that matters most here, and it is undecided.

## Knowledge

**Knows.** How the archive is organised, and why some things are at the back.
**Does not know.** Undecided. `IDEA`
**Suspects.** Undecided. `IDEA`
**False beliefs.** Undecided.

**Secrets.** Whether she has any is `IDEA` and should stay that way for a while.
Rule R3 says nobody has the full picture, and that includes her.

## Arc

**Arc possibilities.** From gatekeeper to ally. From gatekeeper to obstacle. Or:
she has been quietly maintaining something for thirty years without ever calling
it that.

**Romantic.** No. Faculty.
**Friendship.** Something like it, eventually, on her terms.
**Antagonistic.** The most likely form: she is not hiding anything, and she still
will not let that box leave the room.

## Constraints

**Never do.** Be sinister. Loom. Appear at the end of a corridor.
**Never say.** Anything knowing or portentous. If she turns out to know
something, it lands in one flat sentence, once.
