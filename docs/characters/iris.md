# Iris

**Status:** `PROVISIONAL`
**Data file:** `data/characters/iris.json`

## Function

**Role**
Student volunteer on the archive digitisation project. Has been running it
essentially alone since March.

**Narrative function**
The game's relationship to *evidence as feeling*. She is the reason the archive
is a place with a person in it rather than a puzzle box, and she is the one whose
private practice turns out to matter.

## Interior

**Public personality**
Brisk, funny in a way that arrives slightly before you expect it, apparently
unbothered. Answers questions with the actual answer. Has been alone with this
job long enough to have stopped noticing.

**Private personality**
Careful to the point of superstition about objects. Photographs everything before
cataloguing it — nine thousand photographs of things nobody has asked about —
because cataloguing turns a thing into a line and she cannot stand it. She has
never described this to anyone as anything but a quirk.

**Core desire.** For things to be looked at properly before they are filed.
**Core fear.** That she will file something that mattered and never know.
**Core wound.** Not yet decided. `IDEA` — something she did not photograph.
**Core contradiction.** She trusts photographs more than people, and she is
lonelier than anyone in the story.

**The lie she tells herself.** That the second index is just being thorough.
**What she actually needs.** Someone else to have seen it too. Not to be
believed — to be *accompanied*.

**Values.** Accuracy. Attention. Not being precious about it out loud.
**Boundaries.** Being handled carefully. She will take an object out of your
hands and apologise, and mean the apology and not the taking.

## Voice

**Speech patterns.** Complete sentences, occasionally two clauses too long, then
a short one that lands. Finishes a conversation by going back to work rather than
by saying anything.

**Vocabulary.** Precise about physical things, vague about feelings, and aware of
the asymmetry. Says "the maybe pile" without irony.

**Humour.** Dry, self-directed, deployed to move past something.

**Topics avoided.** March. Why nobody else is on the project. Whether she minds.

**Under stress.** More precise, not less. Retreats into procedure.
**When comfortable.** Talks about objects at length, which is how she talks about
herself.

## Relationships

**Iris → player.** Starts at nothing and moves on `trust` and `familiarity`
before `attraction`. She has to see you handle something carefully first.

**Iris → Theo.** Mutual respect, mutual irritation. He wants the ledger to be
right; she wants the object to be seen. They are the same argument from two
sides and neither has noticed.

**Iris → Ms. Ellery.** Complicated. Ellery gave her the job and then left her
alone with it, and Iris has not decided whether that was trust or disposal.

## Knowledge

**Knows.** The contents of box seventeen better than the list does. That the
loose photograph is on no list; she has checked three.
**Does not know.** What the stamp says. She has not turned it over with gloves
on, and she will not do it carelessly.
**Suspects.** That the maybe pile is not random.
**False beliefs.** None yet. `IDEA` — a well-placed one here would be strong,
since she is the character the player will trust first.

**Secrets.** The second index. Not hidden exactly, but never mentioned, which
after eight months amounts to the same thing.

## Arc

**Arc possibilities.** From private practice to shared one. Or: her carefulness
turns out to have been the thing keeping the situation stable, and she has to
choose whether to keep doing it.

**Romantic.** Slow, quiet, built on shared attention rather than declaration. The
scene where it turns is not a confession; it is her showing someone the folder.
Would be unbearable if rushed.

**Friendship.** Easier than romance and nearly as good. She needs a colleague
more urgently than she needs a partner.

**Antagonistic.** If the player is careless with the archive — or with her — she
does not shout. She stops explaining, and takes the boxes back.

## Constraints

**Never do.** Panic. Handle an object roughly. Say something cruel and mean it.
**Never say.** Anything that explains her own psychology. She does not have that
vocabulary about herself and acquiring it would be the end of her arc, not the
middle.
