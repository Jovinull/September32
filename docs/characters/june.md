# June

**Status:** `PROVISIONAL`
**Data file:** `data/characters/june.json`

## Function

**Role**
Student. Transferred in two years ago. Not from the town, not new either.

**Narrative function**
The sceptical reading, given a person and good reasons. Rule R5 in
`docs/canon/PHENOMENON_RULES.md` says an ordinary explanation must stay available
and the player must never feel stupid for holding it. June is how.

## Interior

**Public personality**
Warm, quick, sociable — the easiest person in the cast to talk to. Deflects with
charm so smoothly that people do not notice being deflected.

**Private personality**
She wants this to be nothing, urgently, and the urgency is not about the archive.

**Core desire.** For this place to be ordinary.
**Core fear.** That somewhere being ordinary is not something you get to choose.
**Core wound.** Whatever she transferred away from. Not decided. `IDEA`
**Core contradiction.** She is the most sociable person here and the only one who
arrived alone.

**The lie she tells herself.** That she is being reasonable.
**What she actually needs.** For it to be safe to be interested in something.

**Values.** Proportion. Not making things worse by naming them.
**Boundaries.** Being told she is in denial. She will agree pleasantly and leave.

## Voice

**Speech patterns.** Fluent, quick, changes subject well. Sentences arrive
finished.

**Vocabulary.** Casual, warm. The one character who uses contractions freely and
jokes first.

**Humour.** Frequent and generous — and, used as deflection, the tell.

**Topics avoided.** The transfer. Where she was before. Anything that requires the
world to be strange.

**Under stress.** Funnier. Watch for the joke that arrives too fast.
**When comfortable.** Genuinely curious, and it is a relief to see.

## Relationships

**June → player.** `familiarity` moves fastest of anyone in the cast. `trust` is
the slow one, and does not follow from it.

**June → Theo.** Likes him, finds his certainty restful, will not follow him into
the ledger.

**June → Iris.** Slightly afraid of her, and could not say why.

## Knowledge

**Knows.** Nothing about the archive. She has not been in the room.
**Does not know.** That the sceptical position is going to become untenable.
**Suspects.** That the others are enjoying this, and that enjoying it is a luxury.
**False beliefs.** Not about the phenomenon — about herself. She believes her
scepticism is a position rather than a defence.

**Secrets.** Why she transferred. `IDEA`

## Arc

**Arc possibilities.** From defence to choice: she stops needing the world to be
ordinary and becomes able to look at it. Or she is right, and the cost of being
right is that nobody wanted her to be.

**Romantic.** The easiest to start and the hardest to deepen. Getting past the
charm is the whole relationship, and it should take most of a playthrough.

**Friendship.** Immediate, and only becomes real much later.

**Antagonistic.** She does not fight. She becomes unavailable, warmly, and the
player may not notice for days.

## Constraints

**Never do.** Be a fool. Be proven trivially wrong. Be the comic sceptic.
**Never say.** Anything that makes scepticism look silly. Her arguments must be
the good ones.
