# Mara

**Status:** `PROVISIONAL`
**Data file:** `data/characters/mara.json`

## Function

**Role**
Student. Third generation in the town. Was at the gate on a Sunday because
somebody is always around, and it is usually her.

**Narrative function**
The town's account of itself — the version that was never written down and is
therefore not in the archive. She is the counterweight to the documents, and the
game's argument that a record is not the same as a memory.

## Interior

**Public personality**
Factual to the point of seeming cold. States things without judgement, which
people mistake for judgement. Reliable in a way nobody thanks her for.

**Private personality**
Loyal, and tired of it. Being the one who is always around is a role she was
handed and has never examined.

**Core desire.** For the town to be understood correctly, by someone from
outside.
**Core fear.** That it will be understood correctly.
**Core wound.** Not yet decided. `IDEA` — something her family handles by not
mentioning.
**Core contradiction.** She would defend this place to anyone, and she has been
counting down to leaving since she was fourteen.

**The lie she tells herself.** That she stays because it is easier.
**What she actually needs.** Permission — from anyone — to be from somewhere
without being of it.

**Values.** Accuracy about people. Not gossiping. Showing up.
**Boundaries.** Outsiders being clever about the town. She will go quiet, and the
quiet is the last warning.

## Voice

**Speech patterns.** Flat declaratives. Pauses exactly long enough for you to
notice, then continues. Does not fill silence.

**Vocabulary.** Plain. Never ornamental. Uses "here" where others would name the
place.

**Humour.** Very dry, easy to miss, mostly about the town.

**Topics avoided.** Her family's version of anything. She will say a version
exists and stop.

**Under stress.** Shorter sentences, longer pauses.
**When comfortable.** Tells a story about the town that turns out to be about
herself, and does not appear to notice.

## Relationships

**Mara → player.** `familiarity` moves easily; `trust` does not. Being clever
about the town raises `suspicion` faster than anything else in the game.

**Mara → Iris.** Cautious respect. Iris is doing to the archive what Mara suspects
should not be done to the town: making it legible.

**Mara → Theo.** Finds his certainty about records slightly funny, and slightly
frightening.

## Knowledge

**Knows.** A family account of why the school did not print a yearbook for one
year. `PROVISIONAL` — the account itself is not written yet, and should not be
invented in a scene.
**Does not know.** What is in box seventeen. She has never been in the archive.
**Suspects.** That the digitisation project will produce a version of the town
that replaces the one she was given.
**False beliefs.** Strong candidate for the story's most important one. Her
family's account is probably wrong in a specific, load-bearing way. `IDEA`

**Secrets.** She knows the account. She has not decided whether telling it would
be a betrayal, and that indecision is her whole first act.

## Arc

**Arc possibilities.** From custodian of an unwritten version to the person who
has to choose whether it gets written. Or: she is right, and the archive is doing
something to the town, and nobody believes her either.

**Romantic.** Slow, wary, and specific. She would need to believe you were not
studying her. The turn is her telling you something about the town she has never
told anyone from outside.

**Friendship.** Strong and undemonstrative. She will simply be there.

**Antagonistic.** If the player treats the town as material, she closes. Not
dramatically — she just stops being where you are.

## Constraints

**Never do.** Gossip. Be superstitious. Be a rustic.
**Never say.** Anything that frames the town as quaint, sinister, or a setting.
It is where she lives.
