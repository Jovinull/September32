# Theo

**Status:** `PROVISIONAL`
**Data file:** `data/characters/theo.json`

## Function

**Role**
Student. Keeps the digitisation spreadsheet and re-checks every box against the
accession ledger before signing it off. Nobody asked him to.

**Narrative function**
The game's relationship to *records as data*. He is the one who notices the
anomaly by method rather than by feeling, and he is the reason the player cannot
dismiss it — because he checked ninety-one pages first.

## Interior

**Public personality**
Blunt, brisk, walks fast, produces documents instead of asking. Reads as cold and
is not.

**Private personality**
Checking is how he cares. He has re-checked things for people who never knew he
did it. He is aware this is a strange way to be fond of someone and has not found
a better one.

**Core desire.** For the system to be complete.
**Core fear.** That completeness is a story he tells himself, and the world does
not have it.
**Core wound.** Not yet decided. `IDEA` — something that was filed wrongly and
cost someone.
**Core contradiction.** He trusts the system entirely and is the only person
methodically looking for the place where it fails.

**The lie he tells himself.** That he is being rigorous, not anxious.
**What he actually needs.** To be wrong about something small, safely, in front
of someone who does not make it a lesson.

**Values.** Accuracy. Follow-through. Saying the thing.
**Boundaries.** Being told he is overthinking it. That is the one that lands.

## Voice

**Speech patterns.** Short. Often starts mid-thought, having had the first half
on the walk over. Uses a full stop where most people would use a comma.

**Vocabulary.** Procedural. "Accession number", "column", "signed off". Does not
soften.

**Humour.** Deadpan, rare, usually a precise observation delivered flat.

**Topics avoided.** Why he does this. Whether anyone has noticed.

**Under stress.** More documents. He will bring you a printout.
**When comfortable.** Explains at length, and it is genuinely interesting.

## Relationships

**Theo → player.** Moves on `respect` first. He notices whether you do what you
said you would; a kept promise is worth more to him than a kind word.

**Theo → Iris.** Respects her and does not understand her. Her photographing
things before cataloguing them reads to him as an unlogged step in a process,
which is nearly the worst thing he can think about someone.

**Theo → Mara.** Wary. She knows things about the town that are not written
anywhere, which he finds structurally unsettling.

## Knowledge

**Knows.** That one ledger column has no date header. That fourteen items sit
under it, eleven of them in box seventeen, two in the maybe pile, and one a
photograph. That in ninety-one pages nobody has ever forgotten a date.
**Does not know.** What Iris's second index contains, or that it exists.
**Suspects.** That the omission is deliberate. He has not said so, because he
cannot support it.
**False beliefs.** Currently that the system is complete enough for the anomaly
to be a clerical error. He is going to have to give that up.

**Secrets.** He has re-checked volumes I and II end to end, on his own time, and
told nobody.

## Arc

**Arc possibilities.** From "the system is complete" to "the system is a
practice, maintained by people, and people are what it is made of". Or: he
becomes the person who documents this properly, and finds out what documenting it
costs.

**Romantic.** Awkward, honest, entirely without technique. He would tell you
directly and then be unable to look at you. Would suit a player who values being
told the truth.

**Friendship.** He is a very good friend and almost impossible to notice being
one.

**Antagonistic.** Break a promise to him and he does not argue. He removes you
from the process, politely, and does the work himself.

## Constraints

**Never do.** Exaggerate. Claim more than the evidence supports. Perform emotion.
**Never say.** "I just have a feeling." He would rather say nothing, and does.
