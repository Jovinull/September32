# September 32 — Act 1 playtest: the long questionnaire

The longer version, for anybody who has the time. About twenty-five minutes.

**Please do the short one first, or instead.** If you only fill in one, fill in
[`PLAYTEST_QUESTIONNAIRE_QUICK.md`](PLAYTEST_QUESTIONNAIRE_QUICK.md) — a
complete short form is worth more than an abandoned long one.

**Answer in order and do not skip ahead.** Some questions are deliberately asked
before others, and reading section D before answering section B changes the
answer to section B.

Free text everywhere. There are no scales except where a scale is genuinely the
question. Write as little or as much as you like; one honest sentence beats a
paragraph written to be helpful.
