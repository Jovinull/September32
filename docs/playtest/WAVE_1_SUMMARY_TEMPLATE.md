# Wave 1 — summary

One copy for the wave, assembled from the per-tester analyses.

**Do not fill any of this in with invented or illustrative data.** Every number
here will be quoted later by somebody who has forgotten where it came from. An
empty field is a field nobody has answered yet; a plausible field is a lie with a
long half-life. If a row cannot be filled, write `not established` rather than a
guess.

**Fill this in after all the per-tester analyses, not alongside them.** Reading
tester five's answers while writing the summary of testers one to four is how a
wave turns into a confirmation of whatever the first two people said.
