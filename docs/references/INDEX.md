# Reference index

Original analysis of published work, written to extract **principles**. Every
entry states what to take and what not to.

## The copyright rule

`CANON`, and it is not negotiable.

**Do not download or store:** game scripts, text dumps, extracted assets, fan
translations of commercial works, or ripped files. Not for research, not for
"analysis", not temporarily.

**Do write:** original analysis based on official material, developer
interviews, published criticism, documentation, and having played the thing.

**Never reproduce:** characters, names, dialogue, scenes, structures specific to
one work, twists, lore, or protected terminology.

The distinction that matters: *"Higurashi makes you love a village before it
becomes dangerous"* is a principle. A scene of children at a festival before a
murder is a copy. Principles are how everyone learns to write; copies are theft
and, worse, produce a game that is about another game.
