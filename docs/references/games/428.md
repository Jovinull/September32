# 428: Shibuya Scramble

Original analysis. No content from the work is reproduced here.

## USE FOR

**Causality between people who never meet.** One person's small decision
changes another person's situation elsewhere, and the player sees both sides.

**Consequence as the core pleasure.** Not "what happens next" but "what did I
just cause".

**Multiple perspectives on one situation**, where each is incomplete.

## DO NOT COPY

The live-action presentation. The specific structure and jump mechanic. Any
character. The Shibuya setting.

## PRINCIPLE

> The most interesting consequence of a choice is one that lands on somebody the
> player was not thinking about.

## RELEVANCE TO SEPTEMBER 32

Directly informs the NPC-to-NPC relationship graph. Relationships are directed
and any pair can hold a vector, so `iris->theo` is representable today even
though the prototype barely uses it.

It also informs how commitments should feel: breaking one with Theo should send
Theo somewhere, and that somewhere should matter.

## RISKS

**Point of view.** Its cross-cutting requires showing scenes the protagonist is
not in, which this game currently cannot do — an open question with real engine
consequences (`docs/canon/OPEN_QUESTIONS.md`).

**Combinatorial cost.** Cross-character causality multiplies the states that need
content. The simulator exists partly to keep that honest.
