# The House in Fata Morgana

Original analysis. No content from the work is reproduced here.

## USE FOR

**Recontextualisation as the primary emotional device.** Understanding what was
already shown, differently. The reveals are not new information; they are new
sense.

**Patience with tragedy.** It takes the time necessary and does not rush a
feeling for structure's sake.

**Memory as unreliable and sincere at once.** People misremember without lying.

**Restraint in prose.** Its most devastating passages are plain.

## DO NOT COPY

The setting, the structure, any character, the specific tragedies, the framing
device.

## PRINCIPLE

> The strongest reveal is not new information. It is the reader understanding
> something they already had.

## RELEVANCE TO SEPTEMBER 32

Everything about how the archive should work. A document seen in hour two,
meaning something else in hour twenty, without a retcon — that is the target, and
it requires setups that were not flagged as setups.

The prose restraint is the calibration in `docs/style/HORROR.md`: plainer
sentences as the temperature drops.

Its treatment of misremembering-without-lying is directly what `false_belief`
models.

## RISKS

**Length and linearity.** Its effect depends on a long, controlled sequence. A
storylet system does not control order, so recontextualisation has to be built on
things the player will *definitely* have seen — which means anchors carry it, not
storylets.
