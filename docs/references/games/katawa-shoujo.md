# Katawa Shoujo

Original analysis. No content from the work is reproduced here.

## USE FOR

**Characters who exist independently of the romance.** They have interests,
friendships and problems that do not involve the protagonist, and would continue
without him. This is the single most transferable thing about it.

**Slice-of-life as substance.** Long stretches of nothing happening, in which
everything happens. The reader is not waiting for the plot.

**Specificity over sensitivity.** Its characters are individuals first. The thing
that could have defined them is instead one true fact among many.

**Romance built from proximity and attention** rather than from a meter.

## DO NOT COPY

Any character. The school. The route structure — its five discrete routes are
exactly what this project is not doing. Its specific subject matter, which is not
this game's to handle.

## PRINCIPLE

> A character the player would want to spend an afternoon with, with no plot
> attached, is a character the plot can afford to threaten.

## RELEVANCE TO SEPTEMBER 32

The prototype's cast is written to this standard. Iris has a private practice
that predates the player and would continue without them. Theo re-checks ledgers
nobody asked him to. Mara was at the gate because somebody always is.

The character bible template's **narrative function** field enforces it: if two
characters answer it identically, one of them is not needed yet.

## RISKS

**Route thinking.** Its structure is the thing this project explicitly rejects
(`docs/canon/PREMISE.md`). Borrowing its warmth while keeping the storylet
architecture means resisting the pull toward "Iris content" as a block.

**Length.** Its slice-of-life works at visual-novel length. A day-cycle game has
to make each individual day pleasant, which is harder.
