# Raging Loop

Original analysis. No content from the work is reproduced here.

## USE FOR

**Failure as acquisition.** Dying gives the player something concrete, and the
game says so explicitly. The loop is a knowledge economy.

**Explicit keys.** The player can see what they have learned and that it is
progress. Making the currency visible removes the frustration from repetition.

**Deduction the player performs.** It trusts them to work things out and rewards
it.

## DO NOT COPY

The village, the ritual, the specific loop rules, any character, the folklore
framing, the "keys" terminology itself.

## PRINCIPLE

> If a run ends badly, the player should end it holding something they can use.
> Otherwise it was not a run, it was a waste.

## RELEVANCE TO SEPTEMBER 32

`persistent.s32_meta["meta_clues"]` is the same idea at a much smaller scale:
things the player has earned across runs, used sparingly to gate a rare option.

Its explicitness is the part worth taking. Its scale is not — this game has no
looping in fiction, so the equivalent of a "key" is much quieter.

## RISKS

**Making the loop the subject.** Rule R7 keeps the loop out of the fiction
entirely.

**Overusing it.** `docs/narrative-design/META.md` budgets a handful of meta
options for a whole game. Five would turn the game into a hunt for brackets.
