# Roadwarden

Original analysis. No content from the work is reproduced here.

## USE FOR

**State-driven text.** Where you are, what time it is, what you have done — all
change what is available and what it says.

**Routine as structure.** A day cycle that is the game rather than a frame for it.

**Information as currency.** Knowing something is what opens things.

**Text that respects its own limits** and does not pretend to be a bigger game.

## DO NOT COPY

The setting, the character, the specific systems, the survival elements.

## PRINCIPLE

> Location and time are not scenery. They are gates, and gating on them makes a
> small world feel large.

## RELEVANCE TO SEPTEMBER 32

The world model is essentially this: seven time slots, locations, weather, and
storylets gated on all three. `s32_engine.world.locations_with_content` is the
lesson it teaches indirectly — a state-driven game must let the player *find* the
content, or the systemic richness is experienced as an empty road.

That was not a theoretical point here: the simulator's dead-end rate went from
81% to zero when the model of a player stopped wandering at random.

## RISKS

**Busywork.** Its routine has purpose. A day cycle without one is a chore, and
the ambient storylets exist to make ordinary days feel like days rather than a
queue — which the content-mix report is there to verify.
