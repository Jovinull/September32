# The Letter

Original analysis. No content from the work is reproduced here.

## USE FOR

**Consequence across characters.** A choice made as one person lands on another,
who is also playable.

**Scale of state.** Many small variables producing genuinely different outcomes.

**Perspective as information.** Seeing a situation from two sides changes what
both mean.

## DO NOT COPY

The premise, the curse, any character, the chapter structure.

## PRINCIPLE

> When the player later controls the person their earlier choice affected, the
> consequence stops being a number and becomes an experience.

## RELEVANCE TO SEPTEMBER 32

Reinforces the same open question as 428: whether this game ever shows a scene
the protagonist is not in. A cheaper version is available and probably better —
the player learns what their choice did by *hearing about it*, from the person it
happened to.

## RISKS

**Combinatorial explosion.** Many variables across many perspectives is exactly
what the simulator exists to keep honest, and exactly what a smaller project
should be cautious of.
