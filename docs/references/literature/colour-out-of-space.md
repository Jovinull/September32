# The Colour Out of Space

**H. P. Lovecraft, 1927.** Author died 1937.

**Public-domain basis:** published before 1930 (public domain in the United
States), and more than 70 years after the author's death (public domain in
life+70 jurisdictions). Verified 2026-08-30 — see
`docs/references/literature/PUBLIC_DOMAIN.md` for the corpus policy.

Original analysis. No text from the work is reproduced here.

## USE FOR

**Gradual contamination of the ordinary.** The change is agricultural,
domestic, mundane — crops, water, animals — which makes it far worse than any
monster.

**A phenomenon genuinely outside description.** The prose admits it cannot name
the thing, and means it.

**A retrospective account** from someone who has been to the place.

**Slow, physical, cumulative decline** rather than an event.

## DO NOT COPY

The meteorite, the farm, the family, the colour itself, and the author's wider
mythos and terminology. His prejudices are not incidental to his work and are not
being borrowed.

## PRINCIPLE

> Contaminate the ordinary. A well going wrong is more frightening than anything
> that could climb out of it.

## RELEVANCE TO SEPTEMBER 32

The escalation model in `docs/narrative-design/PACING.md` is this shape: the
change arrives in the ordinary things — a stamp, a column, a photocopy — rather
than in a new element.

"Outside description" is what R4 protects. The phenomenon has no form because
giving it one would make it a thing, and a thing can be looked at.

## RISKS

**Cosmic scale.** His horror is indifferent and vast. This one is institutional
and small, and inflating it would break the register.

**Imitating the voice.** His prose style is very particular and would fight the
understatement `docs/style/WRITING_STYLE.md` asks for.
